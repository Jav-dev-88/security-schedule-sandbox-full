    Leave settings · Types & holidays
    Leave types

    To record leave, click the guard’s name on the weekly schedule → Attendance → Leave / attendance type. LOA means Leave of Absence. Custom types cover the full selected shift, including both parts of linked shifts.

    Vacation dates

    Pick Monday to automatically fill Sunday (7 days inclusive). You can change the last day for a longer or shorter vacation. All of this guard’s shifts on these days become Blank shift — needs coverage. Saved vacations also apply when you create new weeks; the Master stays unchanged.
