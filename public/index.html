Edit directly in the worksheet

    Guards on leave this week

    OT preview uses payroll worked hours above 40 per guard per week. Paid leave, holidays and manual pay adjustments are excluded. OT is part of worked hours, not added again to total paid hours.

    Click a cell and start typing a guard’s name. The remaining letters fill automatically and are highlighted. Known names save automatically when you finish typing or click outside. New names become temporary guards when you click outside. Quick Add Row uses the platoon’s normal hours and leaves the post blank. Green = paid holiday, off duty · Purple / Covering = assignment differs from Master · Blue = billable (blue marker on billable coverage) · Gray = blank shift · Amber = Blank shift needing coverage. Click a post or time to edit it directly; click outside to auto-save. Hours recalculate. Changes to row times apply to all seven days. Existing rows keep their positions; quick rows are added at the bottom of their platoon.

    Hours & Billing Preview

    Payroll Preview

    Payroll adjustments

    Hours and notes only. These change payable hours without changing schedule rows or billable hours. For an existing sick entry, update its pay status in Leave & Coverage instead of adding the same pay twice.

    Payroll transfers

    Keep a shift visible but count its hours in a different payroll week. Destination weeks can be saved before their schedules exist.

    Worked hours are split between posts, never counted twice. Extra post hours are not automatically overtime. Confirm attendance and paid leave before payroll.

    Leave & Coverage

    Click a staffed weekly cell and choose Attendance to record leave, a late arrival or an early departure.

    Vacation list

    Pick Monday to automatically fill Sunday (7 days inclusive). You can change the last day for a longer or shorter vacation. All of this guard’s shifts on these days become Blank shift — needs coverage. Saved vacations also apply when you create new weeks; the Master stays unchanged.

    Staff

    Use Remove to take a guard out of new assignment lists. Saved schedules and payroll history are kept. Removed guards can be restored. A new name typed directly in a cell is saved as a temporary guard.

    Deleted Rows — saved images

    Changes and deleted-row pictures save to the shared database. Every schedule save keeps a version backup. Sunday overnight hours stay with the Sunday week ending.
